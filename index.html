Chronology

Fortnite story timeline

S1

The Beginning

The Island emerged as a mysterious battlefield. The earliest days featured a simple map and a growing sense that something far larger was at play beneath the surface.

S2

Storm and Secrets

The Zero Point became central to the story. The Island began to react to the reality-warping force at its heart, and the Storm started to feel like a living entity rather than a simple hazard.

S3

Fate and Chaos

The narrative deepened with a new battle for control of the Island, bringing in major cosmic events and a darker understanding of what the Zero Point really was.

S4

Planetary Destruction

The Island was nearly torn apart by a massive meteor strike. Standing at the heart of the chaos was the battle between the heroes of the Island and the forces trying to exploit the Zero Point.

S5

The Last Reality

The story expanded with Prop Disguises, hidden truths, and a growing sense that the Island had become a watchtower for the entire multiverse. The lines between worlds blurred.

S6+

Heroes, Aliens, and Dimensions

From the rise of The Seven to giant cosmic threats, Fortnite began weaving together ancient myth, superhero lore, alien invasions, and reality-breaking conspiracies.
